About

A visual way to understand AI and Machine Learning

AI Model Playground is an educational platform designed for students, researchers, educators, and beginners who want to understand what happens inside common AI and Machine Learning models. Instead of only reading theory, users can interact with model inputs and parameters and observe the effect visually.
