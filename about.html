ABOUT AUTHOR

Najib Ur Rehman

Najib Ur Rehman is an engineer, researcher, and academician with experience spanning both industry and academia. His professional interests include engineering, emerging technologies, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and the development of practical digital solutions.

Through his academic and professional work, he aims to bridge the gap between theoretical concepts and real-world applications, with a particular interest in making technical knowledge more accessible, interactive, and easier to understand.

The AI Model Playground reflects this approach by providing a simple and interactive environment where users can explore different machine learning concepts, observe how models behave, and better understand the processes behind them.
